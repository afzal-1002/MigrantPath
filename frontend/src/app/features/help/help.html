Help

Short answers to how this service actually works.

How the assessment works

You answer a short set of questions about your situation (citizenship, current status in Poland, and what you're trying to do). Your answers are evaluated against the same real, published eligibility rules our team maintains for each procedure - never guessed or generated on the fly.

How recommendations work

Once your assessment is complete, we check it against every procedure we currently cover and show you which ones appear relevant based on your answers. A recommendation is never a guarantee of eligibility or an official decision - it's a starting point, based on official sources.

How case tracking works

Starting a case from a recommendation gives you a checklist of the steps, documents, and fees typically involved. We don't store or upload your actual documents - the checklist just tracks whether you consider each item ready, so you can see your own progress at a glance.

Why official sources matter

Every procedure we show cites the real government source it's based on (legislation, the Office for Foreigners, a Voivodeship Office, or a Warsaw municipal source). Rules change, so always check the linked source for the current, authoritative requirement before relying on anything here.

Updating your answers

If your situation changes, you can review and continue an assessment, or start a new one - see your dashboard.

Privacy and your data

You can export a copy of your data or permanently delete your account at any time from your account page. See our Privacy Policy for the full detail.

Independence

This is an independent informational service. We are not the Polish government and do not provide legal advice - see our Disclaimer.
