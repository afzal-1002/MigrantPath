Cookie Policy

Last updated: reflects the state of the application as of Phase 11.

We use exactly two cookies. Both are strictly necessary for the site to function - we don't use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies of any kind.

CookiePurposeLifetime
JSESSIONID Keeps you logged in between requests. Session (until you close your browser, or your session expires).
XSRF-TOKEN Protects your account from cross-site request forgery attacks. Contains no personal data. Session.

Consent

Because both cookies are strictly necessary for the site to work at all (there's no way to log in without them), we don't show a cookie-consent banner. If we ever add a cookie that isn't strictly necessary, we'll ask for your consent before setting it.
