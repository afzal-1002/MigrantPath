What this service is

Foreigner Warsaw is an independent, informational guided-eligibility and case-tracking tool for foreigners handling administrative procedures in Warsaw, Poland. It is not a government service and not a substitute for legal advice from a qualified immigration lawyer or an official government office. See the Disclaimer page for the full statement on this.

What we provide

A guided questionnaire that suggests which administrative procedures may apply to your situation, based on rules encoded from official Polish government sources (Polish legislation, the Office for Foreigners, the Migrant Info Point, Mazowieckie Voivodeship Office, and gov.pl), and a personal checklist to help you track your progress through a procedure you choose to pursue.

What we don't provide

  • We do not file anything on your behalf, and we are not a party to your immigration case.
  • We do not guarantee a recommendation is complete, current, or correct for your exact circumstances - always confirm requirements with the relevant official office before relying on them.
  • We do not provide legal advice. Nothing on this site is a substitute for consulting a licensed immigration lawyer.
