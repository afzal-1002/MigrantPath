Nothing on this site constitutes legal advice. The guided questionnaire suggests which procedures may be relevant to your situation based on rules we've encoded from official sources, but it cannot account for every individual circumstance. For a decision that matters to your legal status, consult a licensed immigration lawyer or the relevant government office directly.

How our recommendations are generated

Every recommendation you see comes from a deterministic, database-driven rules engine - a fixed set of conditions written and reviewed by us, sourced from official government publications. We deliberately do not let an AI model decide eligibility or generate legal requirements: if AI is ever used anywhere on this site, it is only to explain or summarize content that a human has already reviewed and approved, never to invent or decide it.

Immigration law and procedure requirements change. We do our best to keep published content current and sourced, but we cannot guarantee it reflects the very latest change the moment it happens. Always verify time-sensitive details (fees, deadlines, required documents) with the official source before relying on them.
