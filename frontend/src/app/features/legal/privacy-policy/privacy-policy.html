Privacy Policy

Last updated: reflects the state of the application as of Phase 11.

Foreigner Warsaw is an independent informational service. It is not operated by, or affiliated with, the Polish government, the Office for Foreigners (UDSC), the Mazowieckie Voivodeship Office, or the City of Warsaw. See the Disclaimer page for more on this.

What we collect

  • Account data - your email address, password (stored only as a one-way hash, never in plain text), and name, so you can log in and we can address you by name.
  • Assessment answers - the guided-questionnaire answers you give (e.g. nationality group, purpose of stay, income band, family situation), used to determine which procedures may apply to you.
  • Recommendation history - which procedures were suggested to you and why, so you can review your own results later.
  • Case and checklist progress - if you start tracking a procedure, we store which documents/steps you've marked complete.
  • Technical/security logs - IP address, browser user agent, and timestamps of requests, used only for security monitoring and debugging. We never log your password, tokens, or assessment answers.

What we deliberately do not collect

  • No payment or billing information (we don't have a payments feature).
  • No government ID, PESEL, or passport numbers.
  • No document uploads.
  • No advertising or analytics tracking of any kind.

Who can see your data

Only you can see your own assessment, recommendation, and case data. Our administrators can manage the legal content (procedures, rules, sources) published on the site, but have no interface that lists or browses individual users' assessments or cases.

How long we keep it

We currently retain account, assessment, and case data for the life of your account. We do not yet have a fully self-service "delete my account" feature - if you want your data deleted, contact us and we will do it manually. Backups are kept encrypted, off-instance, and on a rolling retention window; our audit trail of admin actions on published legal content is kept permanently for accountability, but it does not contain your personal data.

Cookies

We use two strictly-necessary cookies (session login and CSRF protection) and no tracking or advertising cookies. See the Cookie Policy for details.

Contact

This is a pre-launch project without a dedicated support address yet. Once the product has a real public launch, this section will be updated with a real contact channel for privacy requests.
